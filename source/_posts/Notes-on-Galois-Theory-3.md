> Galois理论部分习题笔记, 包括Lang的Algebra

$\mathrm{Ex.\ 5.1.}$ $$(\alpha^2+\alpha+1)(\alpha^2+\alpha)=\alpha^4+2\alpha^3+2\alpha^2+\alpha=-2\alpha$$考虑到 $(\alpha^3+\alpha^2+\alpha+2)-(\alpha-1)(\alpha^2+2\alpha+3)=5$ , 因此 $(\alpha-1)^{-1}=-(\alpha^2+2\alpha+3)/5$ .

$\mathrm{Ex.\ 5.2.}$ $F(\alpha^2)$ 上, $\alpha$ 满足 $\alpha^2-x^2=0$ , 因此 $[E:F(\alpha^2)]\leqslant 2$ . 如果 $E\ne F(\alpha^2)$ , 则 $\alpha\not\in F(\alpha^2)$ , 因此 $[E:F(\alpha^2)]=2$ , 与 $[E:F]$ 为奇数矛盾.
